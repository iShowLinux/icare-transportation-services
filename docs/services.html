Our Services

Comprehensive non-emergency medical transportation tailored to every patient's needs.

Service 01

Ambulatory Transport

Our ambulatory transport service is designed for patients who can walk independently but need a reliable, comfortable ride to and from their medical appointments.

We provide true door-to-door service — our driver comes to your front door, assists you to the vehicle, and walks you into your appointment. No curb drops. No shortcuts.

  • Door-to-door assistance
  • Uniformed, professional driver
  • On-time pickup guarantee
  • Standby wait service available
  • Return trip coordination
Book Ambulatory Transport
Service 02

Wheelchair Transport

Our wheelchair-accessible vehicles and trained drivers ensure that patients with mobility challenges receive the same level of safe, dignified transportation as any other passenger.

Drivers are trained in proper wheelchair securing, safe boarding and deboarding, and handling any equipment that travels with the patient.

  • ADA-compliant accessible vehicles
  • Trained wheelchair securing
  • Medical equipment accommodation
  • Safe ramp boarding & deboarding
  • Extra time allotted — never rushed
Book Wheelchair Transport
Service 03

Facility & Private Pay

iCare partners directly with assisted living communities, independent living facilities, adult day centers, and skilled nursing facilities to provide recurring, reliable transportation for their residents.

We also serve private-pay individuals who need flexible, on-demand scheduling without the restrictions of insurance-based programs.

  • Facility partnership programs
  • Recurring daily / weekly runs
  • Private pay — no insurance required
  • Recreational & social outings
  • Flexible scheduling
