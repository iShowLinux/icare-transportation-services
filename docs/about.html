About iCare

A veteran-owned company built on service, trust, and genuine care for every passenger we transport.

Our Story

More Than a Ride — A Commitment to Care

iCare Transportation Services was founded with a single purpose: to make sure every patient in the South Carolina Lowcountry can get to their medical appointments safely, on time, and with dignity.

We understand that missing a doctor's visit or a dialysis session can have serious consequences. That's why iCare operates with the reliability and discipline that comes from our military roots — showing up, every time, no excuses.

As a fully insured, veteran-owned business certified by the South Carolina Public Service Commission and the Office of Regulatory Staff, we hold ourselves to a higher standard because our passengers depend on it.

Get in Touch

Proudly Veteran Owned & Operated

The values that define our military service — punctuality, accountability, and commitment to those in our care — are the same values that drive every iCare transport. We bring that same discipline to ensuring our passengers reach their destinations safely and on time.
